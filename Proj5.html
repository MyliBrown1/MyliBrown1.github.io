ERP Simulation Project

Ran a simulated cereal manufacturing company in SAP as a team of four, competing across four rounds of sourcing, pricing, and production decisions.

Working with three teammates, our team (“ERP Meusli”) played the role of a cereal manufacturer based in Germany. We decided what products to produce and sell based on our manufacturing capacity, supply and demand, and target markets. Across four rounds of twenty days each, we procured materials, manufactured products, and sold into the available markets — deciding what to produce, how much to order, how to pay down our initial loan, and when to upgrade our manufacturing line.
